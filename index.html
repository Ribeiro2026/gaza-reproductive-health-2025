2.2 Monthly Number of Deliveries by Maternal Age Group

Analysis of the delivery data reveals a highly centralized age distribution. The 19-40 year-old age group constitutes the overwhelming majority, accounting for 89.8% to 96.1% of monthly deliveries, indicating that childbearing is predominantly concentrated within this standard reproductive age range.

Conversely, deliveries in the under-18 and over-40 groups represent a small minority. Notably, the 16-18 age group shows a noticeable rise in June (8.3%), suggesting variable influences on this demographic. The More than 40 group remains a small but persistent segment, while the "Less than 16" group is consistently negligible.

The data confirms a stable maternal demographic centered on the 19-40 age group, with minor fluctuations in younger and older cohorts that may warrant further investigation.

2.3 Deliveries by Governorate

Geographically, more than one-third (35.8%) occurred in Gaza Governorate, followed by Khan Yunis Governorate with 34.9%, and 27.5% in the middle zone. The lowest percentage of deliveries reported was 1.8% in North Gaza Governorate which corresponds to the large population displacements from the North due to the evacuation orders and large-scale destruction; there were no functional hospitals in North Gaza from May 2025 until the end of the reporting period.

From the monthly trends, the number of deliveries began to decline from February, while an increase occurs in Gaza and North Governorates as a result of the return of the displaced people to these governorates after starting truce, then decrease suddenly in August in Gaza governorate as a result of Gaza invasion while it begun to increase in Khan Yunis and Middle zone.

2.4 Number of Deliveries by Place of Delivery

A total of 98.4% of reported deliveries took place in hospitals, while 1.6% occurred outside health facilities. Cases of emergency or precipitate deliveries that happened outside hospitals, the mothers and newborns were immediately or shortly thereafter brought to the hospital for examination and official registration. Consequently, all neonates regardless of delivery location were registered at the hospital.

2.5 Number of Deliveries by Mode of Delivery

Normal vaginal deliveries accounted for 72.4% of all births, while 3.8% were assisted vaginal deliveries (using vacuum or ventouse). Cesarean sections represented 23.8% of total deliveries, of which 60% were planned and 40% were emergency sections.

Although this cesarean rate exceeds the WHO's recommended range of 10–15%, it remains lower than the Ministry of Health (MOH) rates recorded in Gaza between 2020 and 2022, when cesarean deliveries showed an upward trend, reaching over 27% in 2022. Cesarean sections performed for infertility are no longer observed due to the closure of all infertility facilities.
